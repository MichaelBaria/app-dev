# My Favorite Series: Regular Show

## Overview
**Regular Show** is an animated comedy series created by J.G. Quintel for Cartoon Network. It follows two best friends who work as groundskeepers at a park and keep getting pulled into absurd, over-the-top adventures while trying to slack off.

## Why I Love It
- Hilarious *slice-of-life* humor that spirals into chaos
- Great mix of comedy, music, and 80s/90s nostalgia
- Characters who feel like real friends
- Episodes that start ordinary and end with cosmic stakes

## Main Characters
1. Mordecai (a blue jay)
2. Rigby (a raccoon)
3. Benson (the gumball machine boss)
4. Skips (the immortal yeti)
5. Pops (the cheerful lollipop-like manager)
6. Muscle Man (the loud park worker)
